# Archive

<div className="dcv-page-intro">

A lot of the early Direct Connect web has gone offline over the years: wikis, forums and mailing lists where the protocol and its software were worked out. We rebuilt several of them as faithful, read-only mirrors so the knowledge stays reachable. More on the effort in the [forum thread](https://forum.dcvault.net/t/archiving-and-preservation-of-content-related-to-dc).

</div>
